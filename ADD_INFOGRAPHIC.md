# Adding the AI Value Roadmap Infographic

## Quick Instructions

To display the "From Hype to ROI: A Leader's AI Value Roadmap" infographic on the landing page:

### Step 1: Save the Image
Save the infographic image with the filename: **`ai-value-roadmap.jpg`**

### Step 2: Place in Public Folder
Copy the image file to: **`public/ai-value-roadmap.jpg`**

### Step 3: Refresh
Refresh your browser - the infographic will appear at the top of the landing page!

---

## Alternative: If You Don't Have the Image Yet

The landing page will automatically show a placeholder with the key information from the infographic:
- Three stages: Strategize & Plan, Build & Scale, Measure & Govern
- The Four Categories of AI Value

Once you add the actual image file, it will replace the placeholder automatically.

---

## Image Requirements

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Filename**: `ai-value-roadmap.jpg` (exact name)
- **Location**: `public/` folder in the project root
- **Recommended size**: 1200px wide or larger for best quality

---

## Troubleshooting

**Image not showing?**
1. Check the filename is exactly: `ai-value-roadmap.jpg`
2. Check it's in the `public/` folder (not `src/` or elsewhere)
3. Hard refresh your browser: `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac)
4. Check browser console for errors (F12)

**Image looks blurry?**
- Use a higher resolution image (at least 1200px wide)
- Ensure the image is saved at high quality
